Marketing Resource Management (MRM) software exists because modern marketing teams need more than basic project tracking. As teams grow, channels multiply, and campaigns span regions, marketers need a way to connect strategy, people, content, and execution in one system. For small teams, spreadsheets or lightweight project tools may be enough.
