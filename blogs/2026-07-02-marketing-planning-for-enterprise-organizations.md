Marketing planning for enterprise organizations is the process of turning business strategy into coordinated marketing execution across teams, channels, regions, campaigns, budgets, and stakeholders. In a small marketing team, planning may happen in a few meetings, spreadsheets, briefs, or project boards. But in an enterprise organization, marketing planning is much more complex.
